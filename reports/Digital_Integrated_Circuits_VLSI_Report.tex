\begin{abstract}
This paper synthesizes five structured VLSI design modules spanning SPICE device characterization across 180\,nm, 90\,nm, and 32\,nm Predictive Technology Models (PTM/TSMC) and physical mask layout in a $0.5\,\mu\text{m}$ SCMOS process. In Module~1 (PTM 180\,nm, $V_{DD} = 1.8\,\text{V}$), square-law parameters ($k_n' = 201\,\mu\text{A/V}^2$, $k_p' = 60\,\mu\text{A/V}^2$) and threshold voltages are extracted alongside PMOS sizing sweeps. Module~2 characterizes a 4-bit Carry-Lookahead Adder (CLA) in TSMC 90\,nm LVT ($V_{DD} = 1.0\,\text{V}$) from $0^\circ\text{C}$ to $100^\circ\text{C}$, showing worst-case carry propagation rising from 237.86\,ps to 264.83\,ps and average power increasing from $171.53\,\mu\text{W}$ to $186.33\,\mu\text{W}$. Module~3 benchmarks Static CMOS (6 core transistors; $t_{pHL} = 9.2544\,\text{ps}$, $t_{pLH} = 8.6947\,\text{ps}$) against Differential Cascode Voltage Switch Logic (DCVSL, 14 core transistors; $t_{pHL} = 13.8884\,\text{ps}$, $t_{pLH} = 22.7624\,\text{ps}$) and mitigates charge sharing in dynamic 3-input NAND gates via a pull-up keeper transistor in PTM 32\,nm. Module~4 verifies \texttt{AOI21} Elmore delay bounds, transmission-gate dynamic register transitions ($\Delta t_r = 154.9\,\text{ps}$), and pipelined memory timing closure at $T = 300\,\text{ps}$ (1.67\,GHz). Finally, Module~5 implements full-custom \texttt{NAND4} and 4-stage shift-register physical layouts in Tanner L-Edit ($0.5\,\mu\text{m}$ SCMOS) and extracts post-layout parasitic netlists.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
As CMOS feature sizes scale from sub-micron ($0.5\,\mu\text{m}$ and 180\,nm) down to deep-nanometer regimes (90\,nm and 32\,nm), digital integrated circuit performance becomes increasingly sensitive to carrier mobility degradation, short-channel velocity saturation, internal dynamic charge sharing, and interconnect parasitic capacitances~\cite{rabaey}. This work documents a unified simulation and physical design flow using Synopsys HSPICE and Tanner Tools (S-Edit and L-Edit) completed at the School of Electrical and Computer Engineering, University of Tehran.

\section{180\,nm Parameter Extraction \& 90\,nm CLA Thermal Analysis}
\subsection{Device Characterization (PTM 180\,nm)}
Using Synopsys HSPICE at $V_{DD} = 1.8\,\text{V}$ (\texttt{Q1\_1}--\texttt{Q4}), $I_D$--$V_{DS}$ sweeps across $V_{GS} \in [0.4, 1.0]\,\text{V}$ isolate square-law transconductance and channel-length modulation:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{NMOS (PTM 180\,nm):} $k_n' = 201\,\mu\text{A/V}^2$, $\lambda_n = 1.484\,\text{V}^{-1}$, $V_{th,n} = 0.394\,\text{V}$.
    \item \textbf{PMOS (PTM 180\,nm):} $k_p' = 60\,\mu\text{A/V}^2$, $\lambda_p = 0.900\,\text{V}^{-1}$, $V_{th,p} = 0.277\,\text{V}$.
\end{itemize}
Diode-connected body-effect sweeps (\texttt{Q2\_1.sp}) and PMOS width sweeps (\texttt{Q4.sp}, $w_2 \in [0.1, 3.0]\,\mu\text{m}$ at $V_{in} = 0.8\,\text{V}$) quantify switching-point tunability.

\subsection{4-Bit CLA Thermal Scaling (TSMC 90\,nm LVT)}
A 4-bit Carry-Lookahead Adder (CLA) was evaluated across $0^\circ\text{C}$, $25^\circ\text{C}$, and $100^\circ\text{C}$ at $V_{DD} = 1.0\,\text{V}$ (\texttt{Delay\_*.sp} and \texttt{power\_*.sp}, integrated over $0$--$3.4244\,\text{ns}$). As summarized in Table~\ref{tab:cla}, carrier mobility degradation dominates over $V_{th}$ reduction at $V_{DD} = 1.0\,\text{V}$, monotonically increasing both critical path delay ($t_{pLH}(C_{out})$) and average power dissipation.

\section{32\,nm Logic Family Benchmarking \& Dynamic Keeper Design}
In Module~3 (PTM 32\,nm, $V_{DD} = 1.0\,\text{V}$), Static CMOS (6 core transistors) and DCVSL (14 core transistors) were compared on propagation delay, static power, and switching energy (Table~\ref{tab:families}). Static CMOS achieved lower delay ($9.2544\,\text{ps}$ $t_{pHL}$, $8.6947\,\text{ps}$ $t_{pLH}$) and lower dynamic switching power ($1.648\,\mu\text{W}$/$2.491\,\mu\text{W}$ vs.\ $17.123\,\mu\text{W}$/$17.036\,\mu\text{W}$ for DCVSL).

For dynamic 3-input NAND gates (\texttt{CA3-Q2.sp}), internal node charge redistribution produces voltage droop for $t \ge 40\,\text{ns}$; inserting a small PMOS feedback keeper (\texttt{Mbl} in \texttt{E\_NAND3}) restores full-rail logic integrity without static short-circuit contention.

\section{Complex Gates, Dynamic Register \& Physical Layout}
\subsection{Sequential Timing \& Pipelined Memory (PTM 32\,nm)}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{\texttt{AOI21} Elmore Modeling:} Referenced to a minimum inverter ($\approx 4.2\,\text{ps}$), analytical Elmore delay estimates yield $17RC \approx 23.8\,\text{ps}$ ($t_{pLH}$) and $21RC \approx 29.4\,\text{ps}$ ($t_{pHL}$).
    \item \textbf{Dynamic TG Register:} Output $v(q)$ transitions $10\%$--$90\%$ from $6.1852\,\text{ns}$ to $6.3401\,\text{ns}$ ($\Delta t_r = 154.9\,\text{ps}$); clock-skew margins were stress-tested with a 5-inverter \texttt{CLK\_bar} chain.
    \item \textbf{Memory Pipeline (\texttt{MEMORY.sp}):} Operates reliably at $T = 300\,\text{ps}$ ($1.67\,\text{GHz}$) while exhibiting setup violations at $100\,\text{ps}$ and $200\,\text{ps}$.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Full-Custom SCMOS Physical Layout (Tanner L-Edit)}
Using Tanner L-Edit and S-Edit with $0.5\,\mu\text{m}$ MOSIS SCMOS design rules (\texttt{MHP\_N05.TDB}, $L = 6\lambda$), physical layouts were constructed for a 4-input \texttt{NAND4} cell and a 4-stage serial \texttt{ShiftRegister}. Netlist extraction (\texttt{NAND4\_extracted.sp}, stimulated at $V_{DD} = 1.0\,\text{V}$) explicitly captures layout parasitics including $C_{par}(V_{DD}) = 1.212\,\text{pF}$ and $C_{par}(\text{OUT}) = 636.86\,\text{fF}$.

\section{Conclusion}
Across four CMOS technology generations ($0.5\,\mu\text{m}$ down to 32\,nm), experimental HSPICE and Tanner L-Edit workflows verify the interplay between thermal mobility loss in 90\,nm arithmetic blocks, area/power overhead in dual-rail DCVSL, keeper stabilization against charge sharing, clock-race sensitivity in dynamic sequential elements, and layout-induced capacitive loading.
